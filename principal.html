Shahid Mukhtar Chishti

Principal

Dear Students, Parents, and Visitors,

Welcome to The Lahore City College Zafarwal! It is a matter of great pride and privilege for me to lead an institution dedicated to academic excellence and moral integrity.

Our goal is not just to impart textbook knowledge, but to build a strong character, foster critical thinking, and prepare our students to face the challenges of tomorrow with confidence.

We believe in a strong partnership between teachers, students, and parents. Together, we can create an environment where every student discovers their true potential.

Best wishes for a bright future!
